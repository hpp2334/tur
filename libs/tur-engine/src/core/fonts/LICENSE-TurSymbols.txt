Tur Symbols Regular — the engine's built-in platform-symbol fallback slice.

Provenance: `TurSymbols-Regular.ttf` is a SUBSET of DejaVu Sans (the glyphs
for U+2190-2193, U+21E5, U+21E7, U+2318, U+2325, U+232B, U+23CE only),
renamed to "Tur Symbols" as the license below requires for modified copies.
DejaVu changes are in the public domain; the typeface originates from
Bitstream Vera. Source: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/

The subset covers the small common platform-symbol set that the bundled
Roboto faces lack (notably U+2318 PLACE OF INTEREST SIGN — the playground
status bar's "⌘S to run"), installed by `core::fonts` as a last-resort face
on the generic families.
